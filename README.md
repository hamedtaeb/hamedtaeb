## How I Think About AI & Data Solutions

I believe successful AI and data products require more than building a model. They need:

- A clearly defined business problem
- Reliable and well-prepared data
- The right success metrics
- Strong model evaluation
- Product thinking around users, workflows, and adoption
- Feedback loops after launch
- Monitoring, iteration, and continuous improvement

The questions I usually ask are:

- What business or user problem are we solving?
- What decision will this model or analysis improve?
- What data do we need, and how reliable is it?
- What does success look like?
- Which metric matters most for the business?
- How will users interact with the output?
- How will we monitor performance and improve over time?

This mindset helps me work across product, data, engineering, and business teams to move from idea to execution.
